I am a research scientist at Qualcomm AI Research in Amsterdam, where I lead a team of three
working on RL for LLM post-training, efficient reasoning, and looped language models.
I introduced the Conditional Information Bottleneck view of budget forcing (NeurIPS 2026) and
a looped-transformer design that cuts the KV cache of Ouro models by 4x.

I came to machine learning from physics. I did my PhD at the University of Bologna and
Columbia University, building Monte Carlo simulations and statistical analyses for the XENON
dark-matter experiments, after a summer on CMS data at CERN. Before Qualcomm, I was a researcher at
ISTI-CNR in Pisa and a visiting researcher at USC's Information Sciences Institute,
working on face recognition across resolutions and the detection of adversarial attacks.

I review for NeurIPS, ICML and ICLR, and I am a co-inventor on more than ten US patents and
applications.
